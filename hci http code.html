ISRO Successfully Deploys Next-Gen
NavIC Satellite to Boost Regional GPS
Navigation
The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO)
successfully launched the NVS-01 navigation
satellite aboard GSLV-F12 from Sriharikota.
Weighing 2,232 kg, the spacecraft is the first in
India's next-generation NavIC constellation.
Featuring an indigenous atomic clock and civilian
L1 frequency signals, it significantly boosts
precision positioning, aviation tracking, and
emergency response capabilities across the South
Asian region.

Sample editorial
Designing calmer news interfaces
Prototype Takeaway
News reader interfaces benefit when layout
density is restrained, typography scales
comfortably, and readers retain direct control over
bookmarks and navigation paths.
Information fatigue often arises not from the quantity
of dispatches, but from competing visual priorities.
When headlines, banners, audio badges, and
animated widgets vie for attention on a single
viewport, comprehension degrades rapidly.
In this prototype exploration, layout choices prioritize
calm readability. A consistent 20px margin protects
content from edge clutter, while safe areas ensure
system indicators remain distinct from user
interactions.
Predictable bookmarking and clear back navigation
support confidence during brief sessions. By paring
back nonfunctional decorative toggles, readers can
focus entirely on understanding concise summaries
at their own pace.

mobile reading flow and interface clarity.
